Privacy and data

Which personal data we process, why, on what basis, for how long, who receives it and what your rights are. We follow the GDPR and Belgian law.

Who receives your data?

Email and domain: Combell (Belgium). Hosting: GitHub Pages (Microsoft, US), which may briefly log your IP address and time. Form: the email service Brevo (France) and the host of the form route in the EU. Bookings: Cal.com. Accounting: our accountant. Authorities: only where the law requires.

We never sell data and use no advertising or analytics trackers. Our processors are bound by agreements on security and use.

Outside the European Economic Area

Wherever possible we choose EU providers. Cal.com and GitHub may process data outside the EEA; their privacy information explains on what basis. We transfer no other data outside the EEA.

Security

The connection is encrypted (https). The form stores nothing in your browser and the form route neither keeps nor logs the content of your message. Access to our mailbox and systems is limited.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability of data you provided, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email info@platformaat.be; we reply within one month.

Not satisfied? Contact us first. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, contact@apd-gba.be).
