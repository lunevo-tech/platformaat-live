How we work

Direct contact and progress you can see. You always know what we’re building, why, and what comes next.

After your first message

  1. You tell us what’s stuck

    Through the form or the calendar. A few sentences are enough.

  2. A 15-minute intro

    Online, no strings attached. You’ll hear whether and how we can help.

  3. A fixed-price proposal

    Scoped, with a timeline. Work starts only once you agree.

During the project

  1. Understand

    Goals, users and how things run today. What already works well?

  2. Build and test

    In short steps. You try real progress and give feedback.

  3. Keep improving

    After delivery: maintenance, support and extensions as agreed.
