Why a small software company?

You don’t need an IT department, just someone who listens and delivers.

  1. You talk to the builder

    No sales rep or project manager in between. Your request goes straight to the person who builds it.

  2. Start small

    A first part at a fixed price, so you can see it works before you spend more.

  3. What’s built stays yours

    Code, domain and data are in your name. If you want to continue with someone else later, you can.

What happens when you get in touch?

No obligation. You know the next step in advance.

  1. You tell us what’s stuck

    Through the form or the calendar. A few sentences are enough.

  2. A 15-minute intro

    Online, no strings attached. You’ll hear whether and how we can help, even if the answer is that someone else is a better fit.

  3. A fixed-price proposal

    Clearly scoped, with a timeline and what you keep. Work only starts once you agree.
