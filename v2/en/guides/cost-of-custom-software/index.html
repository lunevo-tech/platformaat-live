What does custom software cost?

Pricing lists starting prices. Here’s what drives the final price, and how to spot an honest quote.

What drives the price

  • The number of screens and steps.
  • How many integrations are needed and how well the tools allow them.
  • Whether data must move over from an old system.
  • The number of users and permissions.
  • Whether AI or reporting is added.

Keeping it affordable

We start with a small, usable first part. You see the value before deciding on the next one. Hosting and maintenance are listed separately in the quote.

An honest quote…

  • says what’s included and what isn’t;
  • names who owns the code and the data;
  • explains what happens when something changes;
  • lists hosting, maintenance and third-party licences.

Tell us what’s stuck

A first conversation costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You get an honest answer, even if it is “we’re not the right fit”.
