Custom or off-the-shelf software: how to choose

Sometimes one, sometimes the other, and often both. Here’s how to check for yourself.

Choose off-the-shelf if…

  • the way you work matches what the package does;
  • you have no unique steps that set you apart;
  • you want to start quickly with little development;
  • a per-user subscription stays cheaper than building.

Choose custom if…

  • your staff work around the package because it falls short;
  • your way of working is an advantage you don’t want to give up;
  • you combine data from several tools by hand;
  • you depend on a vendor who can change prices.

The middle ground

A package for accounting, your own tool for what is unique, and integrations in between. Often the most sensible mix.

Write down the five steps that take the most time and check whether your package supports them. If many are missing, custom or a mix pays off.
