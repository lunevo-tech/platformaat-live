What is an internal platform, and do you need one?

Not a big ERP and not a website: one tool for your own team where scattered work comes together.

What it gives you

  • Everything in one place instead of scattered.
  • Recurring tasks that run automatically.
  • New staff who find their way faster.
  • You change how you work without waiting for a vendor.

When it makes no sense

If you’re a team of two and everything sits in one package that works fine. Building a platform because you can is not a reason. If a package will do, we say so. See also custom or off-the-shelf.

Tell us what’s stuck

A first conversation costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You get an honest answer, even if it is “we’re not the right fit”.
