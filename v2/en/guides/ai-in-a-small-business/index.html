AI in a small business: what really helps?

Much of what you see is demo, not daily work. Still, AI can already take over dull tasks, if you pick the right ones.

Where AI works well

  • Reading documents and pulling out the key data.
  • Drafting a reply that a staff member checks.
  • A first draft of a text or translation.
  • Searching your own documents.

Where it works less well

For decisions with big consequences and for work that must be right without checking. An AI can be confidently wrong, which is why a person always checks.

Where to start

Pick one recurring task where a mistake isn’t serious and is quick to check, such as preparing order emails. Measure whether it really saves time. See also AI at PlatforMaat.
