Website, platform or app: what do you need?

The words get mixed up, but they’re not the same. It depends on who uses it and for what.

The difference

Company website
For people who want to find and assess you. Fast, readable on a phone, clear.
Online shop
For buying online: payments, stock, shipping and consumer rules.
Web platform or portal
For a fixed group that logs in: staff, customers or suppliers.
Mobile app
For people on the move. A web app on the phone is often enough, cheaper and without an app store.

How to choose

Start with the question: who uses it and what must they be able to do? Often it’s a mix, like a good website plus a small portal.
