Your data and the GDPR in custom software

Having software built also means having data stored. What we arrange up front. This isn’t legal advice.

Whose and where?

The data is yours and exportable. It’s hosted in the EU; provider and region are in the quote. Every quote comes with a data processing agreement: purpose, security, retention and what happens if something goes wrong.

What you arrange yourself

  • Knowing which personal data you process and why.
  • A privacy notice for customers or staff.
  • A record of processing, if it applies to you.
  • Retention periods.

And with AI?

We explain which data is processed where and whether it leaves the EU. We don’t process sensitive data without a clear agreement. For your own situation: a lawyer or the Data Protection Authority.

Tell us what’s stuck

A first conversation costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You get an honest answer, even if it is “we’re not the right fit”.
