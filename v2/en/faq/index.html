Frequently asked questions

Short, honest answers. If your question isn’t here, just ask.

Answers

What does PlatforMaat do?

We build custom software and portals, websites, online shops and apps, AI tools and integrations between software, and we take care of hosting and maintenance.

Who do you work for?

Small and mid-sized companies in Belgium. Industries shows the problems we see per sector. Other companies are welcome too.

What does it cost?

You get a quote with a fixed price, no hourly billing. Starting prices are on Pricing.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. We usually start with a small, usable first part. The timeline is in the quote.

Do I need to replace my current software?

Not necessarily. Often we connect what you have and only build what’s missing.

What if you’re no longer available one day?

That’s why we agree up front what you receive: your data and, for software built for you, the code, documentation and access. Another developer can then take over. We can’t guarantee a replacement.

Who owns the code and the data?

The data is yours and you can always export it. For software built specifically for you, the code is yours too.

Where is my data stored?

On EU hosting. Provider and region are in the quote, together with a data processing agreement.

Do you use AI?

Where it helps, for example to prepare email or documents. You always know when you’re dealing with AI, and a person checks.

Can I hire you just for a website?

Yes. A website, shop or app is a full project in its own right.

What if you can’t help?

We’ll tell you honestly, and where possible what you could do instead.

Who is behind PlatforMaat?

PlatforMaat is a sole proprietorship in Antwerp (Hoboken). Details are under Company details.

Tell us what’s stuck

A first conversation costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You get an honest answer, even if it is “we’re not the right fit”.
