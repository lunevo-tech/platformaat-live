Advice, analysis and design

Before we build anything, we look together at what happens today and what you actually need. Sometimes building isn’t the answer, and we’ll tell you.

How it goes

  1. Map the work

    We follow how work comes in, who picks it up and where it gets stuck.

  2. What’s feasible

    What software, integrations or AI can do, and what is better solved another way.

  3. Design and prototype

    Screens in your branding and a version you can already click through.

What you get

A scoped plan and a fixed-price quote. The process scan (half a day together, with a report of three quick wins) starts at €450 and is credited against a follow-up project. See Pricing.

Tell us what’s stuck

A first conversation costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You get an honest answer, even if it is “we’re not the right fit”.
