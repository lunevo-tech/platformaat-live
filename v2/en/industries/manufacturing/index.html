Software for manufacturers

Orders from quote to delivery in one overview, with a shop-floor screen and time and material tracking.

What often goes wrong

  • An order exists as an email, a spreadsheet row and a paper work order, each slightly different.
  • Not everyone on the floor knows what has priority.
  • Time and material per order are estimated afterwards.
  • The office can’t quickly say when an order will be ready.

What we can build

Order overview
One shared version from quote to delivery.
Shop-floor screen
Today’s work orders, in the right order.
Tracking
Time, material and quality checks entered by the worker.
Link with accounting or ERP
For invoicing and stock.

Questions

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. We build the missing piece and connect it to what you already use, as far as that software allows.
