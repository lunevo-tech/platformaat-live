Software for installers

For HVAC, plumbing and electrical firms: quotes, job sheets, scheduling and service contracts in one place.

What often goes wrong

  • Quotes are put together by hand from supplier price lists, right when you are busiest.
  • Job sheets come back on paper and are retyped for the invoice.
  • Service contracts with an expiry date live in one person’s head.
  • Extra work gets done but not recorded, so it isn’t invoiced.

What we can build

Quoting tool
Price lists, discounts and your margin together, with a quote in your branding.
Job sheet app
Photos, materials and the customer’s signature, straight into your admin.
Scheduling
Who is where and what is still free.
Service calendar
Reminders for customer and office, so no contract quietly lapses.

Questions

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. We build the missing piece and connect it to what you already use, as far as that software allows.

What if our team is small?

Then it stays a small, simple tool. You only pay for what you need, at a fixed price.
