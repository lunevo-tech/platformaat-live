Software for accounting firms

A portal where clients upload documents and see what’s missing, with reminders and document recognition checked by a staff member.

What often goes wrong

  • Clients send documents by email, photo or chat, in every format.
  • Chasing what’s still missing is done by hand.
  • The same questions are asked again every quarter.
  • Documents are recognised, renamed and entered manually.

What we can build

Client portal
Clients upload and see what’s still missing.
Checklists and reminders
Per client and per period, automatically.
AI document recognition
Type, date and amount prepared; a staff member checks.
Deadline overview
Status per client for the whole firm.

Questions

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. We build the missing piece and connect it to what you already use, as far as that software allows.

What if our team is small?

Then it stays a small, simple tool. You only pay for what you need, at a fixed price.
